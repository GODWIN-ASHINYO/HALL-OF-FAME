🏆 Tournament Overview – ACses vs Efb Showdown ⚽🔥

Welcome to the ultimate clash between 🔴 Efb (Premier League) and 🔵 ACses (La Liga)! This tournament brings together 16 players (8 from each group) in a high-energy battle for glory, points, and bragging rights.

⸻

⚡ Format & Fixtures

• Each player faces 4 different opponents (home & away) → 8 matches in total. • Each opponent = 6 points at stake. • Maximum per player = 24 points. • Total points per team = 192 points (Efb & ACses both start here). • After every fixture, the points a player earns are deducted directly from the opponent’s team total.

⸻

🔥 In Summary: This tournament is more than just games — it’s a battle of leagues, a clash of pride, and a chance to grow the community stronger than ever. Play fair, stay active, and let’s make history.
